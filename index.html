Common Questions

Delivery Details

Do you offer same-day or scheduled delivery?

Yes. We offer both same-day delivery and scheduled delivery, depending on availability and your needs. We also offer hot-shot delivery for urgent, time-sensitive requests.

Is there anything outside your delivery limits?

We handle a wide variety of deliveries, including larger and oversized items. For anything particularly large, heavy, or requiring special handling, contact us so we can discuss the details.

Do you deliver outside Regina?

Yes – we provide out-of-town and longer-distance delivery throughout Saskatchewan. Special and longer-distance deliveries can be arranged by request.
